Admissions

Apply to Waypoint Institute

Waypoint Institute welcomes applicants for the 2025 Biblical Formation cohort. Courses are tuition-free, self-paced during the week, and culminate in oral capstones that demonstrate mastery.

  • No tuition, testing, or technology fees
  • Courses delivered in English with translation support for oral work
  • Capstones recorded with 1–2 peers in each class
Start application Talk with our team

Our secure form opens in a new tab. You may save progress and return later.

Eligibility at a glance

Waypoint welcomes adults who are ready to grow as disciples alongside a small cohort.

  • Be 18+ with a secondary completion or equivalent experience.
  • Read and write comfortably in English (oral work may be in your language).
  • Affirm the Apostles’ Creed and Waypoint ethos.

What to prepare

Collect these basics before opening the form so you can finish in one sitting.

  • Your contact information and preferred language.
  • Previous schooling or ministry experience.
  • A short story about your faith journey and goals.
  • Confirmation that you can join Google Meet capstones.

How it works

Three simple steps

Our application mirrors other universities but stays personal and easy to follow.

1

Create your profile

Share your name or approved pseudonym, contact details, and location so we know how to reach you.

2

Share your story

Briefly describe your studies, ministry or work, and how you plan to stay engaged with the cohort.

3

Review and submit

Confirm you can join Google Meet capstones, agree to the Apostles’ Creed, and send the form. You’ll receive a confirmation email.

After you submit

Watch your email for next steps from admin@waypoint.institute.

  • Confirmation: Sent within 24 hours of submitting.
  • Review & interview: We may invite you to a short Google Meet conversation.
  • Decision & onboarding: You’ll receive placement details plus access to the introduction seminar.

Need help at any point? Email us and we’ll walk through the form with you.
