Admissions

Apply to Waypoint Institute

Waypoint Institute welcomes applicants for the 2025 Biblical Formation cohort. Courses are tuition-free, self-paced during the week, and culminate in oral capstones that demonstrate mastery.

  • No tuition, testing, or technology fees
  • Courses delivered in English with translation support for oral work
  • Capstones recorded with 1–2 peers in each class
Start application Talk with our team

Our secure form opens in a new tab. You may save progress and return later.

Before you begin

Review eligibility and gather the basic details that most universities request before you start the application.

  • Applicants must be 18+ or hold a high-school/secondary credential.
  • Instruction is in English; capstone recordings can be in your native language.
  • Plan to devote 8–10 flexible hours per week to each course.
  • Be ready to affirm the Apostles’ Creed and Waypoint ethos.

Timeline & review

Waypoint reviews applications on a rolling basis and communicates decisions by email.

  • Submit by the 15th of any month for priority review that month.
  • Expect a confirmation email within 24 hours of submitting.
  • Interviews, if needed, are scheduled over Google Meet.
  • Decisions include pathway placement and onboarding steps.

How it works

Follow the same steps you expect from other universities

The online form mirrors traditional higher-education applications while keeping the process pastoral and personal.

1

Create your profile

Share your name or approved pseudonym, contact details, country or region, and primary language. This establishes a secure record for the admissions team.

2

Describe your background

List prior schooling, ministry or vocational experience, and any languages you read or speak. This helps us place you with peers who share similar rhythms.

3

Respond to prompts

Write short essays (250–400 words) explaining why you hope to study at Waypoint, how you pursue the Good, True, and Beautiful, and what support you may need.

4

Provide references

Share contact details for a pastor, ministry leader, or mentor who can confirm your readiness. We reach out only after you submit.

5

Confirm technology readiness

Affirm access to the internet, Google Workspace, and the ability to record oral capstones with 1–2 classmates at the end of every course.

6

Sign and submit

Agree to the Apostles’ Creed, our tuition-free pledge, and student policies before sending the application. You can download a PDF copy for your records.

What to gather

Application materials checklist

Collect these items before you begin so you can complete the form in one sitting (about 25 minutes).

Personal details

  • Legal name or protected pseudonym
  • Date of birth and citizenship
  • Mailing address and secure email
  • Preferred languages for reading, writing, and speaking

Academic history

  • Secondary completion or equivalent
  • Prior college or seminary experience (if any)
  • Transcripts or grade reports (optional upload)
  • Relevant certificates or ministry training

Faith & ministry

  • Church or ministry affiliation
  • Summary of current service commitments
  • Short testimony of your walk with Christ
  • Reference contact with email and phone

Technology setup

  • Internet access plan (home, mobile, shared, etc.)
  • Device you will use for coursework
  • Microphone/camera availability for capstones
  • Backup plan for uploading assignments

After you submit

Admissions communicates primarily by email. Add admin@waypoint.institute to your safe list so you do not miss updates.

  • Application review: We prayerfully review your essays, references, and readiness indicators.
  • Interview (if requested): A 20-minute Google Meet conversation to learn more about your goals.
  • Decision: Receive a cohort placement, waitlist update, or guidance on strengthening your application.
  • Onboarding: Accepted students receive access to the Waypoint Introduction Seminar and technology walkthroughs.
