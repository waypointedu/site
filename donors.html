Support Waypoint Institute

Students pay nothing. Your contribution keeps our Christian college certificate tuition-free today and fuels future associate pathways. We are not yet a 501(c)(3); contributions are not tax-deductible.

Where your contribution goes

$500

Build a course

Honorarium for the instructor who authors a reusable course with readings, assignments, and assessments.

  • Funds evergreen curriculum and media
  • Includes initial capstone design
$500

Run a course

Honorarium for the instructor who facilitates a course run, reviews capstones, and offers pastoral feedback.

  • Covers capstone evaluation time
  • Keeps office hours and feedback flowing
Ongoing

Keep access open

Supports translation summaries, technology, and digital library growth so students worldwide can study without tuition.

  • Expands multilingual resources
  • Maintains low-cost platforms and security

How to contribute

We receive contributions directly while we evaluate long-term nonprofit status. Reach out to discuss a one-time or recurring contribution.
